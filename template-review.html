The Breakdown: Pros & Cons

The Advantages

  • Incredible FPS-per-dollar ratio under $200 CAD.
  • 8GB VRAM avoids stutter in modern titles at 1080p.
  • Fully stable drivers on current AMD Adrenalin builds.
  • Responds amazingly to an undervolt + repaste.

The Trade-Offs

  • No hardware Ray Tracing (RDNA 1 architecture).
  • Requires a reputable 550W–600W power supply.
  • Used models usually need fresh paste to avoid hotspot throttling.

6IX PCS Final Verdict

If you are building a budget gaming PC in the Greater Toronto Area with a total budget around $600 to $750 CAD, the RX 5700 XT is hands-down the best graphics card you can buy on the secondhand market. It easily beats the GTX 1660 Super and RTX 2060, while matching newer cards that cost twice as much.
